## Decisions Made

- Paper intent readiness is stricter than scoring readiness. The runtime only creates `OrderIntent` after validation, paper mode, reconciliation, QuantConnect-authoritative portfolio inputs, and risk all pass.
- Reconciliation is evaluated before risk/order intent. A mismatch or missing QuantConnect snapshot blocks new entries and preserves exit/protective obligations.
- Runtime events are emitted as domain evidence only. Telegram delivery remains outside this function and cannot unblock or block safety logic.
