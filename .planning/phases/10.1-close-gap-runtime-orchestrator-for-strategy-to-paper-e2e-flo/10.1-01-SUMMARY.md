## Decisions Made

- Runtime results are evidence contracts only in this plan. They do not execute QuantConnect orders, send Telegram messages, mutate dashboard data, or create backtest artifacts.
- `executed_quantconnect_order_ids` and `quantconnect_fill_ids` are rejected unless QuantConnect-authoritative portfolio state is supplied.
- The default setup registry covers `trend_pullback`, `volume_breakout`, and `relative_strength_leader`; it intentionally excludes `combined_swing`.
